FAQ

Common questions

I've never exercised before. Can I start?

Yes. Most of my clients start as complete beginners. We begin with simple movements and build up at your pace.

Do I need to join a gym?

No. I can design home workouts with little or no equipment. A gym helps for muscle gain, but it's not required for weight loss.

Will I have to stop eating rice and roti?

No. Your diet is built from the food you already eat — we adjust portions and combinations rather than banning foods.

How soon will I see results?

Most people notice better energy in 2–3 weeks and visible changes in 6–8 weeks with consistency. Safe fat loss is usually around 0.5–1 kg per week.

Do you train people outside Jamui?

Yes — through online coaching with video-call assessments, workout videos and daily WhatsApp check-ins.

Is there a female trainer for women?

Yes. Female clients are trained by a female trainer — in person and online. Just choose "Female trainer" in the form or mention it on WhatsApp.

Which languages do you coach in?

Hindi and English. Sessions, diet charts and WhatsApp support are in whichever you are more comfortable with.

I have a health condition. Can you still help?

Often yes, but please share it in the form. For conditions like diabetes, BP, heart issues or pregnancy, I'll ask you to check with your doctor and plan around their advice.
