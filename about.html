My story

Fitness made for real people

I started training because I saw how much better people feel when they get stronger and healthier. But I also saw most plans online didn't fit life here — imported foods, 2-hour gym sessions, diets that ignore your work timings.

So I coach differently. I look at your body type, your routine and your kitchen first. Then I build a plan you can actually follow — and I stay with you every week until you see results.
