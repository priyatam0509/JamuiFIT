Good to know

Most people are a mix

Body types (somatotypes) were described in the 1940s. Experts today agree they rarely exist in pure form — most people are a blend of two, and your type can shift with training and diet.

So I use your body type as a starting point, not a rule. Real adjustments come from tracking how your weight, strength and measurements change week to week.

The three types

How I train each one

Ectomorph

Lean & light

Slim frame, narrow shoulders and hips, finds it hard to gain weight or muscle.

  • Training: heavy compound lifts, moderate reps, longer rest
  • Cardio: light — save energy for growth
  • Diet: calorie surplus, high protein, more carbs, frequent meals
Mesomorph

Athletic

Medium frame, broader shoulders, builds muscle easily — but can gain fat if careless.

  • Training: mixed strength and hypertrophy, varied programs
  • Cardio: moderate, 2–3 days a week
  • Diet: balanced protein, carbs and fats matched to goal
Endomorph

Solid & strong

Wider build, gains fat easily and loses it slowly — but builds strength well.

  • Training: full-body strength + circuits to burn more
  • Cardio: HIIT and daily steps are key
  • Diet: high protein, controlled carbs, slight calorie deficit

General guidance only. Your actual plan depends on your assessment, health history and goals.
