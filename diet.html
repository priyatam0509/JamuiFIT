Quick check

BMI Calculator

BMI is a rough first check of weight for height. For South Asians, health risk starts at lower BMI, so Indian guidelines use 23 as the start of "overweight".

It doesn't measure muscle vs fat — that's why I also check waist and body measurements in your assessment.
