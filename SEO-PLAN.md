# Jamui Fit – SEO Plan (what to do outside the website)

**Be realistic:** nobody can promise #1 on Google. The first realistic win is the
brand search **"jamui fit"**, because no other business uses that name. "gym in Jamui"
and "personal trainer Jamui" are mostly won through **Google Maps (Google Business
Profile) and reviews**, not the website alone. Expect 2–8 weeks for the brand name to
show, and a few months of steady work for the local searches.

Do these steps in order. Tick each one when done.

---

## 1. Put the website live (Day 1)
- [ ] On GitHub, open repo **priyatam0509/JamuiFIT** > **Settings** > **Pages**.
- [ ] Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)`. Save.
- [ ] After 1–2 minutes, open https://priyatam0509.github.io/JamuiFIT/ and check every page and the WhatsApp button.

## 2. Buy your own domain (strongly recommended, ~Rs 500–900/year)
A name like **jamuifit.in** looks professional, is easy to say, and helps Google
match the "jamui fit" search.
- [ ] Buy `jamuifit.in` (or `jamuifit.com`) from GoDaddy, Hostinger, Namecheap or BigRock. Skip paid add-ons (hosting, email) – you don't need them.
- [ ] In the domain's **DNS settings**, add:
  - 4 **A** records, host `@`: `185.199.108.153`, `185.199.109.153`, `185.199.110.153`, `185.199.111.153`
  - 1 **CNAME** record, host `www`, value `priyatam0509.github.io`
- [ ] GitHub > Settings > Pages > **Custom domain**: type `jamuifit.in`, Save. Wait until it says DNS is OK, then tick **Enforce HTTPS**.
- [ ] Ask your web person to replace `https://priyatam0509.github.io/JamuiFIT/` with `https://jamuifit.in/` everywhere (each page's head section, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`). It is written in one marked place per file.

## 3. Google Search Console (Week 1) – tells Google your site exists
- [ ] Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in with the gym's Gmail.
- [ ] Add property:
  - With own domain: choose **Domain**, enter `jamuifit.in`, add the TXT record it shows in your DNS settings.
  - Without domain: choose **URL prefix**, enter `https://priyatam0509.github.io/JamuiFIT/`, verify with the **HTML tag** method (your web person pastes one line into index.html).
- [ ] Left menu **Sitemaps** > submit `sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] **URL Inspection** > paste the home page URL > **Request indexing**. Repeat for Services and Contact.
- [ ] Check once a month: Performance > Queries – see if "jamui fit" appears.

## 4. Google Business Profile – THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP for "gym in Jamui"
This is what puts you on Google Maps and in the map box at the top of results.
- [ ] Go to https://business.google.com > Add business > name exactly **Jamui Fit**.
- [ ] Primary category: **Personal Trainer**. Additional: **Gym**, **Fitness Center**, **Health Consultant** (or Nutritionist if qualified).
- [ ] Address: if clients come to a fixed place, add the real address and place the pin correctly. If you only train at clients' homes/online, hide the address and set **Service area: Jamui** (and nearby towns you really cover).
- [ ] Phone **+91 88628 21060**, website (your domain or GitHub link), hours **Mon–Sat 5:30 AM–9:00 PM**, Sunday closed.
- [ ] Description (750 chars): mention "personal trainer in Jamui", weight loss, muscle gain, diet plans, Hindi & English, female trainer for female clients.
- [ ] Complete verification (video or postcard – do it immediately when asked).
- [ ] Upload **at least 10 real photos**: logo, trainer, training area, sessions (with permission), before/after (with permission). Add 2–3 new photos every month.
- [ ] Add Services with short descriptions (Weight loss, Muscle gain, Diet plan, Online coaching).
- [ ] Turn on messaging / WhatsApp link if offered.

**NAP consistency:** your **N**ame, **A**ddress, **P**hone must be written exactly the
same everywhere – website, Google, Instagram, Justdial, Facebook. Use:
`Jamui Fit` · `Jamui, Bihar 811307` · `+91 88628 21060`

## 5. Reviews (ongoing – biggest ranking factor on Maps)
- [ ] In Business Profile, copy your **"Ask for reviews"** link.
- [ ] Send it on WhatsApp to every happy client. Goal: 10 reviews in month 1, then 3–5 every month.
- [ ] Ask clients to mention what they did, e.g. "lost 8 kg with personal training in Jamui" (never write reviews yourself or pay for them – Google removes them and may suspend you).
- [ ] Reply to **every** review within 2 days, thank them by name; reply politely to bad ones too.

## 6. Instagram @jamuifit (Week 1)
- [ ] Bio: "Personal Trainer in Jamui, Bihar | Weight loss · Diet plans | Hindi & English".
- [ ] Put the website link in the bio link field.
- [ ] Switch to a Business/Creator account; set category "Personal Trainer", add phone, email, and location Jamui.
- [ ] Use "Jamui" in captions and location tags (#jamui #jamuifit #jamuibihar).

## 7. Bing Webmaster Tools (15 minutes, Week 2)
- [ ] https://www.bing.com/webmasters > sign in > **Import from Google Search Console** (one click). Also covers some ChatGPT/Copilot search results.

## 8. Local directories (Weeks 2–4) – free listings, same NAP each time
- [ ] **Justdial** (very important in India) – category Personal Trainer / Gym.
- [ ] **Sulekha** – Fitness trainers.
- [ ] **Facebook Page** "Jamui Fit" – add website, phone, hours.
- [ ] **Apple Maps** (Apple Business Connect) and **Bing Places** (import from Google).
- [ ] Optional: IndiaMART, Yellow Pages India, local Jamui Facebook/WhatsApp groups.
Always add the website link and the same name, address and phone.

## 9. Posting rhythm (keep it simple)
| Where | How often | What |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3–4 posts/week + daily stories | Workout tips, diet tips in Hindi, client results, reels |
| Google Business Profile | 1 post/week | Offer, tip, new photo, "free consultation" |
| Google photos | 2–3/month | Real sessions and space |
| Reviews | Every new happy client | Send review link |
| Website | Every 2–3 months | Update photos, testimonials, prices |

## 10. Monthly 10-minute check
- [ ] Search "jamui fit" and "gym in Jamui" on Google (in a private/incognito window).
- [ ] Search Console: any errors? Is "jamui fit" in Queries?
- [ ] Business Profile: number of calls, website clicks, new reviews.
